What it is
A Mac app that cleans up recordings of worn records and old tapes.

Choose a folder, add your recordings, press Start. There is nothing to set for each record.

What you get

Clean copies, and the care to leave well alone

Cleaner sound

The surface goes, the music stays

  • Clicks, crackle and hiss taken down, as far as they can go without dulling the music.
  • The right treatment for each record. A noisy 78 gets more, a clean transfer is left almost alone.
  • Lossless files with your tags. Every copy is 24-bit Apple Lossless, with the original's tags kept.

Nothing lost

It never touches what you have

  • Your originals never change. Every restored recording is a new file.
  • It tells you when to listen first. A green tick when a copy is done, an amber mark with the reason when you should hear it before you rely on it.
  • It leaves a record alone rather than harm it. Then it writes nothing, and says so.

No work for you

Start it and do something else

  • A long list, left to run. Add files or whole folders. Quit any time: the list goes on when you open the app again.
  • A finished copy of each recording, at an even, comfortable level, for any player, phone or speaker.
  • Silence at the ends, your way. Keep as much of the quiet groove as you like. The music is never cut.

How it works

Three steps

Choose a folder

Pick where the restored copies should go. One of your music folders, if you want to see them in your music app at once.

Add your recordings

Add files or whole folders with Add Files, or drop them on the window. MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, FLAC and more.

Press Start

Each recording is restored in turn. The line at the bottom says what is happening and about how long is left.

Questions

Good to know before you try it

Does it record from my turntable?

No. Shellac & Vinyl works on recordings you already have as files. Transfer the record with whatever you use today, then add the file.

Which files does it read, and what does it write?

It reads MP3, M4A and AAC, WAV, AIFF, FLAC and CAF. It writes 24-bit Apple Lossless (.m4a), named after the original with “(restored)” added, and keeps the original's tags.

What is the difference between the restored copy and the finished copy?

The restored copy is the clean version to keep: the surface taken down and nothing else changed. The finished copy is the same recording brought to an even, comfortable level, so a quiet record and a loud one play alike. It is the one to play. If you only want the restored copies, turn Finished copies off.

Will it dull the music?

It is built not to. For each recording it chooses the gentlest treatment that does the job. When it is unsure, the copy gets an amber mark and the reason, so you can listen before you rely on it. When even the gentlest treatment would harm the music, it writes nothing.

How long does it take?

About half a minute for one side of a 78. A long list can be left to run: the Mac is kept awake until it is done, and the list carries on after you quit and open the app again.

Can I hear before and after in the app?

Not yet. The app has no player: play the original and the copy in your music app to compare. A before and after in the window is on the list of what may come.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No. Everything happens on your Mac, and nothing is uploaded. Only the App Store's own check of your purchase uses the network.
